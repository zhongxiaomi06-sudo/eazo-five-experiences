**Audit date:** 2026-08-27

## Executive decision

The former 6×6 fixture has been replaced by a production-shaped experience: complete 72/30/12 content registries, a 256×144 deterministic Worker, three complete product surfaces, atomic local state, safe Remix, offline support, real Eazo SDK integration, and a result artifact with clear science boundaries. Visual system v2 adds a single-screen mobile laboratory, thumb-reachable control dock, material/tool/notebook bottom sheets, an original transparent specimen hero, and a mobile snap reel of three licensed, transformed science-motion references. All implemented unit, type, build, manifest, and emulated mobile tests pass.

The build is not approved for public production. Four release claims still require evidence that cannot be generated by code: an Eazo-issued App ID plus host acceptance, physical-device performance, named science/safety approval, and target-user comprehension/share-intent results. These are recorded as blockers rather than converted into synthetic PASS evidence.

## Design audit

The visual system follows the project-original Figma direction: midnight navy canvas, fluorescent mint system status, amber granular matter, cyan liquid, compact monospace instrumentation, and generous editorial typography. The first viewport answers what this is, what to do, and what is produced. The simulation stays visually dominant; controls are grouped into transport, tools, material, and notebook rather than generic dashboard panels.

The v2 visual search compared the interaction hierarchy of [Sandspiel](https://sandspiel.club/), [Sandboxels](https://neal.fun/sandboxels/), museum-style interactive exhibits, and [Apple layout guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout). Only structural patterns were adopted: full-bleed simulation, compact mission overlay, persistent thumb dock, and bottom-sheet disclosure. No competitor image, icon set, code, wording, or layout was copied. The generated `matter-specimens-v2.png` is project-original, has alpha transparency, and is registered as `LAB-HERO-002`.

Objective checks pass: safe-area padding, 54 px primary dock targets, 74 px tool targets, visible focus, skip link, reduced-motion override, labeled view modes, no horizontal document overflow at 390×844 or 412×915, readable result hierarchy, and a keyboard-addressable HTML control alternative around Canvas. Mobile Save, Restore, Step, Redo, Clear, Export, and Import remain accessible through the notebook sheet; they were not removed to simplify the viewport. The generated social card reuses the same palette and exact product promise.

Human-only claims are deliberately not signed here. `aestheticLevel=enjoy`, North American copy review, three-second comprehension ≥80%, and share intent ≥60% need named reviewers or target users. Until those records exist, the target may be `S+` but the approved grade remains unset.

## Safety, rights, and privacy

- No copied competitor code, reaction tables, names, UI, screenshots, or trademarks are included.
- Runtime visuals are programmatic; the UI base is project-original. The social card and transparent matter specimen hero are project-generated and recorded in the rights ledger.
- Three six-second challenge-page loops are transformed from Pexels assets `16296848`, `18333329`, and `7126320`. Each source URL, creator, version ID, review date, and Pexels license are recorded as `LAB-REEL-001..003`. The derivatives are cropped, resized, color-adjusted, muted, and embedded as contextual product components; originals are not redistributed. The Pexels license permits free use and modification without mandatory attribution, while prohibiting unaltered standalone resale and implied endorsement.
- The video payload is 802 KB total. Only the first loop receives eager preload; the other two request metadata until the browser needs playback. All clips are muted, inline, looped, and autoplay is disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion` users.
- No real dangerous quantity, concentration, temperature, pressure, apparatus setting, or procedure is supplied.
- Remix imports reject extra fields, scripts, unknown material IDs, oversized payloads, and invalid checksums before mutation.
- No account, camera, microphone, location, advertising, or sensitive personal data is required.

## Eazo audit

The app uses the published, MIT-licensed `@eazo/sdk@0.22.8`, not the old fake adapter. Runtime device context comes from `device`; result sharing calls `share.compose` with a PNG result and a relative target path. On the web or when Eazo does not accept the handoff, the experiment is retained and a local checked Remix is exported.

`VITE_EAZO_APP_ID` is intentionally blank in source control because Eazo must issue it. Therefore “SDK integrated” is PASS, while “accepted by the real Eazo Mobile host” remains BLOCKED. The two statements must not be conflated.
